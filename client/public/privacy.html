Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short:

  • No account, email or phone number is needed.
  • No ads, tracking cookies or analytics tools are used.
  • Your nickname, chat messages and drawings are kept only in the server's memory while the game is running and are deleted when the room closes.
  • If you report a player, the report is kept for a limited time so abuse can be reviewed.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is İbrahim Yeğen (individual developer), contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]. For users in Türkiye, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) applies; see the Turkish notice.

2. What we process and why

Data Purpose Legal basis Retention
Nickname and avatar (color/face) Showing you to the other players in the room Necessary to provide the service In server memory while the room is open; deleted when it closes
Chat messages, guesses, drawings Playing the game Necessary to provide the service In memory during the game; at the end, that game's drawings are shown to the room as a gallery, then deleted when a new game starts or the room closes. Never stored permanently.

IP address and connection data Rate limiting, preventing attacks and abuse, operating the server Legitimate interest (security of the service) Cleared from server memory within minutes; kept for a limited time in the hosting provider's technical logs
Report records: nickname and random ID of the reported and reporting player, the reason, and up to the last 8 chat messages of the reported player Reviewing swearing, insults and inappropriate content; protecting players Legitimate interest (community safety) Kept for a limited time in server logs (the hosting provider's log retention period)

We never ask for or collect your real name, email, phone number, location or contacts. Your secret session key and IP address are not written to report records.

3. Data stored on your device

The game uses no cookies. Your browser's local storage (localStorage / sessionStorage) only keeps: your nickname and avatar, a random session key (per tab in the browser; in the installed app it is kept on the device so you can rejoin your room after the app is closed), the last room code, your sound and language preferences, the players you muted, and the custom word list you entered as a room host. These stay on your device (except the session key and nickname, which are sent to the server when you join a room, and the custom words, which are sent when you set up a room). You can erase all of it with "Delete my data on this device" on the home screen.

4. Sharing

  • Other players in the room see your nickname, avatar, chat messages and drawings (that is how the game works).
  • Hosting provider: the game server is operated by Render Services, Inc. (USA) in its Frankfurt (European Union) data center.
  • Moderation alerts: a copy of player reports may be forwarded to the administrator's messaging channel (e.g. Discord).

If a player saves or shares a drawing from the end-of-game gallery, they do so themselves; the image shows the word and the drawer's nickname.

Your data is never sold or shared for advertising or marketing.

5. Children

The game is meant for a general audience and players can chat with each other. We recommend that children under 13 play under the supervision of a parent or guardian. Swearing is filtered automatically; players can mute and report each other, and the room host can remove a player.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its processing, or ask about who it was shared with, by writing to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We reply within 30 days, free of charge. Since there are no accounts, please include the room code and your nickname. Because in-memory data is deleted when a room closes, in most cases no data will be left to return.

7. Security

Connections are encrypted with HTTPS. Player IDs are random, and secret session keys are never sent to other players. The server rejects malformed or malicious data and enforces rate limits.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and the "Last updated" date changes. For important changes we will also let you know in the game.
